========================================================================
Calm Video Banner Folder for BlogBay Reviews Page
========================================================================

To use your own custom video background for the Reviews page:

1. Place your .mp4 video file in this directory:
   c:\xampp\htdocs\blog_app\videos\

2. Rename the video file to:
   calm_bg.mp4

Full Path:
   c:\xampp\htdocs\blog_app\videos\calm_bg.mp4

Once placed, reviews.php will automatically play your video in the background!
